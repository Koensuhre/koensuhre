# Koen Suhre

Build a bold, professional portfolio website for Koen Suhre — Web Designer, Developer, Multimedia Specialist, Photographer & Videographer (based in the Netherlands), who is positioning himself as a modern Web Designer + Marketer.

GOAL

Create a high-impact, conversion-focused portfolio site that:

- Looks professional but daring and memorable.

- Uses large, bold headings and expressive typography.

- Features smooth scroll animations and micro-interactions.

- Is SEO-optimized from the ground up (semantic HTML, meta tags, Open Graph, fast performance, accessible).

- Has a smart, modern navigation and a standout “Cases / Portfolio” section that highlights projects with a marketing lens.

BRAND & PERSONALITY

- Name: Koen Suhre

- Titles to show: “Web Designer • Developer • Multimedia Specialist • Photographer & Videographer” and subtly hint at “Marketer” as the direction he’s growing into.

- Tone: confident, creative, strategic, no-nonsense, with a touch of boldness.

- Audience: SMEs, agencies, startups, and entrepreneurs who want websites that not only look great but also perform (leads, bookings, brand growth).

VISUAL STYLE

- Style: modern, bold, slightly experimental but still professional.

- Colors: use a striking, high-contrast palette. For example:

  - Base: deep charcoal / near-black background.

  - Accents: electric blue, neon lime, and a warm orange/coral for CTAs.

  - Use color boldly in headings, hover states, and section dividers, but keep body text readable.

- Typography:

  - Headings: a bold, expressive display font (e.g., a heavy geometric sans or a strong grotesque).

  - Body: a clean, highly readable sans-serif.

  - Play with scale: very large H1/H2 on desktop, strong hierarchy, generous line-height.

- Layout:

  - Full-bleed sections, big whitespace, strong grid.

  - Use asymmetry and overlapping elements in hero and case study sections to feel daring.

- Animations:

  - Smooth scroll-based reveal animations for headings, images, and cards.

  - Subtle parallax on hero background or large images.

  - Hover effects on buttons, project cards, and nav items (color shifts, slight scale, underline animations).

  - Keep performance in mind: no heavy libraries unless needed.

SITE STRUCTURE

Create the following pages/sections (single-page scroll or multi-page, but with clear navigation):

1. HERO / HOME

   - Full-screen hero with:

     - Big bold headline, e.g.: “I design & build websites that work as hard as you do.”

     - Subline: “Web Designer • Developer • Multimedia Specialist • Photographer & Videographer” with a small badge or line like “& Marketing-minded creator”.

     - Strong CTA buttons: “Bekijk mijn cases” and “Neem contact op”.

   - Background: abstract geometric shapes or a subtle gradient with bold accent colors.

   - Add a short trust line: “Helping brands in NL grow with strategic web design & content.”

2. ABOUT

   - Section title: “Over Koen” or “About”.

   - Short, punchy bio (3–5 sentences) that positions Koen as:

     - A hybrid creative: design + dev + multimedia + photo/video.

     - Someone who thinks like a marketer: conversion, positioning, storytelling.

   - Include a professional photo placeholder.

   - Add 3–4 bullet points of strengths:

     - “Strategisch denken vanuit marketingdoelen”

     - “End-to-end: van concept tot code en content”

     - “Sterk in beeld: foto, video, motion”

     - “Focus op performance, SEO en conversie”

3. SERVICES

   - Title: “Wat ik doe”.

   - Show 4–6 service cards with icons and short descriptions:

     - Webdesign & Development

     - Branding & Visual Identity

     - Content & Multimedia (foto, video, motion)

     - SEO & Performance Optimization

     - Marketing-minded Web Strategy

     - Ongoing Support & Optimize

   - Each card should clearly hint at business outcomes (more leads, better positioning, stronger brand).

4. CASES / PORTFOLIO (KEY SECTION)

   - Title: “Cases” or “Portfolio”.

   - Subtitle: “Projecten met impact – ontworpen om te converteren.”

   - Layout: large, interactive project cards with hover effects and smooth reveal on scroll.

   - For each project, show:

     - Project name

     - Short tagline (1 line)

     - 2–3 bullet points on what was “slim” from a marketing/design perspective.

     - Tags: e.g. “Webdesign”, “SEO”, “Branding”, “Video”, “Conversion”.

     - CTA: “Bekijk case” (can be a modal or separate page later).

   - Include these three example projects (use placeholders for images):

     A) webagencytwente.nl

        - Tagline: “Een strakke agency-website die vertrouwen en autoriteit uitstraalt.”

        - Slimme keuzes (marketing-instek):

          - Duidelijke positionering als lokale expert in Twente → sterk voor lokale SEO en vertrouwen.

          - Heldere diensten-structuur en CTA’s die bezoekers richting aanvraag sturen.

          - Visueel rustig, met focus op resultaten en cases → bouwt geloofwaardigheid op bij MKB.

        - Tags: Webdesign, SEO, Positioning, Local Business.

     B) heynoona.nl

        - Tagline: “Een expressieve brand-website die verhaal en visueel samenvoegt.”

        - Slimme keuzes:

          - Sterke visuele identiteit die direct het merkgevoel communiceert → onderscheidend in een drukke markt.

          - Storytelling-first opbouw: wie, waarom, wat → emotionele connectie met de doelgroep.

          - Integratie van foto/video-content om het merk ‘leefbaar’ te maken op social en web.

        - Tags: Branding, Multimedia, Storytelling, Visual Identity.

     C) werkgenoten.online

        - Tagline: “Een platform dat matching en community centraal stelt.”

        - Slimme keuzes:

          - Duidelijke propositie voor werkgevers én werknemers → twee doelgroepen slim bediend in één site.

          - Focus op vertrouwen en laagdrempelig contact → belangrijk voor conversie in recruitment.

          - Content-structuur die SEO-vriendelijk is en makkelijk uitbreidbaar voor nieuwe regio’s/diensten.

        - Tags: Platform, UX, SEO, Recruitment Marketing.

   - Add a small note under the cases: “Meer cases op aanvraag – ik werk regelmatig onder NDA.”

5. APPROACH / WERKWIJZE

   - Title: “Mijn aanpak”.

   - 4-step process with icons:

     1. Discover & Strategy (doelen, doelgroep, positionering)

     2. Design & Content (visueel, UX, copy, foto/video)

     3. Build & Optimize (development, SEO, performance)

     4. Launch & Grow (analytics, iteratie, marketing)

   - Emphasize the marketing mindset in each step.

6. TESTIMONIALS (OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED)

   - Title: “Wat klanten zeggen”.

   - 2–3 testimonial cards with:

     - Quote

     - Name, role, company

     - Optional: small logo placeholder.

7. CONTACT

   - Title: “Laten we iets moois maken”.

   - Short intro: “Heb je een project of idee? Laten we kijken hoe we het groot kunnen maken.”

   - Contact form fields: Name, Email, Company, Message, Budget range (optional).

   - Show email address and optionally phone/WhatsApp.

   - Add a small line about response time: “Ik reageer meestal binnen 1 werkdag.”

8. FOOTER

   - Simple, clean footer with:

     - Name: Koen Suhre

     - Short tagline: “Web Design • Development • Multimedia • Marketing-minded”

     - Links: Home, Cases, Over, Contact

     - Social links (placeholders: LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.)

     - Copyright line.

NAVIGATION

- Sticky header with:

  - Logo/text: “Koen Suhre”

  - Nav items: Home, Cases, Over, Werkwijze, Contact

  - CTA button: “Start project”.

- On mobile: hamburger menu with smooth slide-in animation.

- Active state highlighting for current section.

- Consider a “progress bar” at the top that fills as you scroll.

SEO & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Use semantic HTML (header, nav, main, section, article, footer).

- Add proper meta tags:

  - Title, description, Open Graph, Twitter Card.

  - Canonical URL placeholder.

- Use heading hierarchy correctly (one H1 per page, logical H2/H3).

- Add alt text placeholders for all images.

- Ensure fast performance:

  - Optimized image loading (lazy load).

  - Minimal blocking JS/CSS.

- Make the site fully responsive (mobile-first).

- Ensure good contrast and accessibility (keyboard navigation, focus states).

- Prepare content so it’s easy to extend with a blog later (for SEO).

CONTENT LANGUAGE

- All visible content in Dutch (NL), except technical/meta parts where English is fine.

- Tone: professional, direct, slightly bold, no fluffy marketing jargon.

EXTRA DETAILS

- Add subtle background patterns or gradients in some sections to keep it visually interesting.

- Use accent colors for links, buttons, and small decorative elements.

- Ensure all animations feel smooth and premium, not gimmicky.

- Design with the idea that Koen wants to be seen as a “Web Designer + Marketer”, so every section should hint at strategy, conversion, and business impact, not just “mooi design”.

Deliver a complete, ready-to-customize portfolio site with all sections, dummy images, and placeholder text as described above.
